Room area is the exam weight, which the guide gives as the approximate share of scored items from that domain. Open a room for its objectives. Each small square is one objective sheet: dashed means not written yet, outlined means written, filled means you have read it.

Each sheet opens with the objective in the guide's own words. The text then teaches the idea with a worked example and the decision rules the exam relies on, and finishes by showing how the exam tests it. The Remember box beside the text holds what you should be able to recall in two minutes, and Say it back at the end is an optional one-minute check.

The tag on each block, such as §3.9, names the section of the project's study notes that it teaches. Those notes, called the corpus, state each decision the exam can test as a situation, an answer and a reason, and name for each section a discriminator, the one fact that separates the right answer from the tempting one, and the Coverage page tracks which sections are done.

The month plan spreads the 38 sheets over the days you have, adds a review pass, and keeps the last days for the five practice papers. A sheet is marked read when you scroll to its end, and you can untick it.
