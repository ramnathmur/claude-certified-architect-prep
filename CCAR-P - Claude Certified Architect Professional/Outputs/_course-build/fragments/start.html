Room area is the exam weight (the guide's approximate share of scored items). Open a room for its objectives. Each small square is one objective sheet: outlined means unread, filled means you have read it.

What you get from each sheet

  • You know what each objective asks. Every sheet opens with the objective in the guide's own words, so nothing is paraphrased.
  • You recognise the tempting wrong answer. Each sheet ends with how the exam tests the objective, and names the trap family behind every wrong option.
  • You spend time where the points are. The floor plan sizes each domain by its exam weight, from 7% for Developer productivity to 14% for Governance and safety.
  • You finish before the deadline. The month plan fills your days from a start date to your exam date, then keeps the last days for five practice papers.
  • You keep your place. A sheet is marked read when you scroll to its end, and the page offers to continue where you stopped.

How it works

  1. 1Read the sheet. A worked example, then the decision rules the exam relies on, then how the exam tests them.
  2. 2Recall it. The Remember box holds what you should be able to say in two minutes. Say it back at the end is an optional one minute check.
  3. 3Follow the plan. Sheets in the first phase, a review of the Remember boxes in the second, practice papers in the third.

Learn every objective in the order the guide lists it. Meet every trap before the exam sets it.

Questions before you start

Is this an official Anthropic course?

No. It is independent preparation built from Anthropic's Exam Guide v1.0 and a set of study notes written for this project. The Sources page says what each kind of claim rests on.

Which exam does it cover?

Claude Certified Architect – Professional (CCAR-P): 63 items, 120 minutes, closed book, with a pass mark of 720 on a scale of 100 to 1,000. Every objective heading in the course is copied from the guide.

What if I sit the exam after 7 December?

Anthropic has announced that exam content changes on 7 December 2026, and that updated guides publish on 17 November. This course is pinned to guide v1.0. A sitting on or after 7 December is governed by the updated guide, and every sheet would need rechecking against it.

How long does it take?

You choose the dates. The month plan sizes each day by how many study note sections a sheet covers, so a six section sheet gets a day to itself. The default exam date, 3 December 2026, is a placeholder until you enter your booking.

Where is my progress saved?

In this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and you can untick any sheet.

Do the sheets replace practice papers?

No. The sheets teach the objectives. The five practice papers come in the last phase of the plan, sat in Practice Mode.

How was each chapter checked?

By script against the guide and the study notes, then read once by an independent auditor and an independent cold reader. Facts taken from Anthropic's live documentation carry the date they were read. The Sources page lists them.

Nothing to sign up for

There is no account, no email and no payment. The whole course is this one file, and it works offline.
