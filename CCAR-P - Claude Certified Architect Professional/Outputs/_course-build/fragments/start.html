Room area is the exam weight (the guide's approximate share of scored items). Open a room for its objectives. Each small square is one objective sheet: outlined means unread, filled means you have read it.

  1. Read the sheet. It opens with the objective in the guide's own words, teaches the idea with a worked example and the decision rules, then shows how the exam tests it.
  2. Recall it. The Remember box holds what you should be able to say in two minutes, and Say it back at the end is an optional one-minute check.
  3. Follow the plan. The month plan spreads the 38 sheets over your days, adds a review pass, and keeps the last days for the five practice papers. A sheet is marked read when you scroll to its end.

The exam guide is Anthropic's Exam Guide v1.0, effective July 2026, and every objective heading here is copied from it. Anthropic publishes updated guides on 17 November 2026, and a sitting before 7 December is still on v1.0. The Sources page explains the study notes behind each sheet and how each chapter was checked.
