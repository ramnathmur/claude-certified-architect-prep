Pick a start date and your exam date. The plan fills the days in three phases.

In the first you learn the sheets in guide order and sit the section drill for each. Days are sized by how many corpus sections a sheet covers, so a six-section sheet gets a day to itself.

In the second you re-read the Remember boxes domain by domain, heaviest first. In the third you sit Papers 1 to 5 in Practice Mode, one a day, and the day before the exam is rest.

Book before 7 December 2026, when exam content changes. Cancelling or rescheduling closes 48 hours before the appointment.

The default exam date, 3 December 2026, is a placeholder that leaves a few spare days before the content change. Change it to your booking. Your two dates are stored in this browser only.
