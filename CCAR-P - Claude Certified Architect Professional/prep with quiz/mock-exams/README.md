## Why these are named "External", not "MockTest-N"

The eventual official Phase-4 papers will follow the sibling projects' naming —
`CCAR-P_MockTest-N_v1.html`. The `External` prefix on these three keeps that numbering free and makes
it structurally impossible to mistake community-authored content for a corpus-generated, confirmed-weight
paper. **Do not renumber these into the `MockTest-N` sequence when Phase 4 starts** — they stay
`External` permanently, or get moved to `../notes/` with their provenance intact, per the root
`CLAUDE.md` rule that community sources belong in `notes/`, never presented as official material.

## What the external mocks are missing on purpose

Built with a lighter, purpose-specific engine (not a copy of `CCDV-F_MockTest-TEMPLATE_v1.html`, which
assumes a confirmed skill-section map and a RECALL/CONCEPT tripwire specific to that project's own
corpus — neither applies to unofficial content here):

- No RECALL/CONCEPT miss tagging
- No DASHBOARD-DATA.jsonl export
- No EXAM-LOG.md stub generator
- No scaled-score estimate (only a raw X/15 count)

If Ram sits one of these, **do not log it in `EXAM-LOG.md`** — that file is CCAR-P's one source of
truth for standing, reserved for real attempts against confirmed-weight papers.
