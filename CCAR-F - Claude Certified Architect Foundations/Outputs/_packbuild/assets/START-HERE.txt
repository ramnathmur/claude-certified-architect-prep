CCA-F PREP PACK
===============

Open README.html in your browser. That is the whole instruction.

It explains what is in the pack, how to take the practice papers,
and how to track your progress. Everything runs locally in your
browser -- no accounts, no installs, no internet needed.

If your answers do not save between sessions, run open-locally.bat
(Windows, needs Python installed) and use the address it prints.
Some browsers restrict storage for files opened directly from disk.
