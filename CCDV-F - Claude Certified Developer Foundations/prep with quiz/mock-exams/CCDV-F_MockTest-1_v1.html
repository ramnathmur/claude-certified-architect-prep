CCDV-F Mock Exam Template

The first full CCDV-F mock paper - 53 items at the exact published domain weights, transcribed verbatim from the 34 course chapters' own self-tests (Outputs/regeneration/chapters/). Chapters 1-15 are independently gate-reviewed; chapter 16 carries one applied fix with no independent round-2 review; chapters 17-34 are author-only, by explicit decision. Multiple-response items
Every item states its own response count in the stem - that is how the real exam is specified, so the count is the first thing to read. Multiple-response items here are scored all-or-nothing: a majority-right answer scores zero. That is an assumption, not a published fact - the guide does not say. Eight CCAR-F misses came from exactly this leak.
